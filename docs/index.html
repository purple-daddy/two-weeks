Every dated promise we could pin down from Elon Musk, with the deadline he gave and what actually happened. The counter keeps running until the promise ships.

Claims are paraphrased from public remarks, with the date and venue where they were made. A deadline of "this year" or "next year" is counted from the last day of that year. "Late" means it shipped after the deadline; days overdue stop counting on the day it arrived. Status is as of the day you're reading this page. Each entry links to a search for its coverage so you can check the wording yourself.
